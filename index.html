1. Introduction

Welcome to Nurido ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Hoang Dev.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. Nurido is designed as an offline-first, standalone health and nutrition assistant that operates locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nurido processes, how your data is stored locally on your device, how AI features function, and how your privacy is protected when using our mobile application.

By downloading, accessing, or using Nurido, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Storage & Processing

Nurido operates primarily on local device storage to ensure maximum privacy and user control over personal data.

A. No Account Registration Required

Nurido does not require account registration or sign-in. We do not create or maintain user accounts, and we do not collect account credentials such as email addresses or passwords for using the app.

B. Local Health & Profile Data (Stored On Device)

Information you voluntarily enter during setup or in your health profile is stored locally on your device memory (using SharedPreferences and local device storage):

  • Height, weight, and target weight
  • Age and gender
  • Activity level and health goals
  • Calculated daily calorie target, BMR, TDEE, and macronutrient targets (Protein, Carbs, Fat, Fiber)
  • Daily food and meal logs
  • Weight history entries
  • Language preferences and app display settings

C. Meal Photos & Text Submitted for AI Analysis

When you choose to capture a meal photo or send a prompt to Coach AI, that specific content (food photo or text query) is securely transmitted in real-time via encrypted HTTPS to our AI processing endpoint for nutrition calculation.

3. How We Use Information

Your information is used solely to deliver core application functionality, including:

  • Calculating personalized daily calorie, BMR, TDEE, and macro targets
  • Tracking daily food logs and weight progress locally on your device
  • Analyzing food images and providing real-time AI nutrition insights
  • Delivering localized multi-language UI and AI responses
  • Ensuring application stability and responding to customer support inquiries

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. AI Features & Google Gemini API

Nurido utilizes the Google Gemini API to analyze food images and provide nutrition coaching.

When you use AI features:

  • Food photos and text prompts submitted by you are transmitted via secure HTTPS/TLS encryption to process real-time nutritional breakdowns.
  • Data submitted for AI analysis is processed only to generate the requested response.
  • Your personal data is not shared or sold for third-party marketing purposes.

Important Health & Medical Disclaimer

AI-generated nutrition recommendations and food calorie estimates are provided for informational and educational purposes only and do not constitute medical advice.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian before making significant dietary or health decisions.

5. Device Permissions (Camera & Photos)

Nurido requests access to your device's camera and photo gallery only when you choose to scan a meal or select a food photo for AI analysis.

These permissions are entirely optional and can be managed or revoked at any time via your device settings.

6. Premium Subscriptions

Nurido may offer optional Premium features.

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled directly and securely by:

  • Google Play Store (Android)
  • Apple App Store (iOS)

Nurido does not collect, process, or store payment details such as credit card numbers, banking info, or payment passwords.

7. Third-Party Services

Nurido relies on trusted service infrastructure to provide AI analysis and app distribution:

  • Google Gemini API (AI image and nutrition processing)
  • Google Play Services (App distribution and billing)

8. Data Security & Storage

Because your health profile and logs reside locally on your device, their security is tied to your physical device security.

For data transmitted to AI services, we enforce industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption during transit.

9. Data Retention & Local Data Clearance

Since your profile, meal logs, and weight history reside locally on your device, you have complete and instantaneous control over clearing your data at any time:

How to Clear Local App Data:

Open Nurido > Go to Settings > Tap "Clear Local Data" / "Xóa dữ liệu local". This permanently removes your locally stored health profile, meal history, and weight records from your device, returning the app to its fresh initial state.

You can also clear app storage or uninstall Nurido from your device settings to wipe all local data instantly. No email request or waiting period is required.

10. Children's Privacy

Nurido is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on our official Privacy Policy page with a revised Effective Date.
