1. Introduction

Welcome to Nurido ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Hoang Dev.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information Nurido collects, how it is used, stored, and protected when you use our mobile application and related services.

By downloading, accessing, or using Nurido, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Nurido collects only the information necessary to provide its features and improve your experience.

Account Information

If you choose to sign in, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile photo
  • Unique User ID provided by Firebase Authentication

Health & Nutrition Information

Information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Height
  • Weight
  • Target weight
  • Age
  • Gender
  • Activity level
  • Daily calorie goal
  • Nutrition targets (Protein, Carbohydrates, Fat)

Meal Information

When using Nurido's AI features, we may process:

  • Food photos
  • Meal descriptions
  • Nutrition logs
  • Weight history

Device Information

Nurido may automatically collect limited technical information, including:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • Language settings
  • IP address
  • Crash reports
  • Anonymous analytics

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve Nurido's services, including:

  • Calculating personalized calorie recommendations
  • Tracking nutrition intake and weight progress
  • Generating health insights
  • Providing AI-powered nutrition assistance
  • Synchronizing your data securely across your signed-in devices
  • Improving application stability and performance
  • Responding to customer support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

4. AI Features

Nurido uses Google Gemini API to analyze food images and answer nutrition-related questions.

When you use AI features:

  • Food photos and text you submit may be securely transmitted to Google Gemini API for processing.
  • AI responses are generated solely to provide nutrition analysis and dietary assistance.
  • Your personal data is not sold to third parties.
  • We do not use your personal information to train public AI models.

Important Notice

AI-generated nutrition information is provided for informational purposes only and should not be considered medical advice.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making important medical or dietary decisions.

  • Select a photo from your device

These permissions are optional and may be revoked at any time through your device settings.

12. Account & Data Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your Nurido account and all associated personal data, you can request deletion at any time by following these steps:

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to support.nurido@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please send the email from the address registered with your Nurido account so we can verify your identity.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete:

  • Account details (Email address, Display name, Profile photo, Firebase User ID)
  • Health & Nutrition profile (Height, weight, age, calorie targets)
  • All saved meal history, food photos, and nutrition logs

What Data May Be Retained

  • Non-identifiable, aggregated usage logs retained briefly for app functionality/security.
  • Transaction logs maintained by Google Play / Apple App Store for legal and accounting purposes.

All personal data will be completely deleted within 30 days of receiving your request.
