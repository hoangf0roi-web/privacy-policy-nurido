PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: August 18, 2026

App Name: Nurido

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nurido ("Nurido", "we", "our", or "us").

Nurido is a health and nutrition assistant designed to help users track meals, calories, macronutrients, weight, and personal fitness goals. Nurido also provides optional AI-powered food analysis and conversational nutrition assistance.

We respect your privacy and adhere to data minimization principles: we collect and process only the information strictly necessary to provide, maintain, and improve the Application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nurido processes, how that information is used, where it is stored, how third-party services are involved, and how you can manage or delete your data. By using Nurido, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Information You Provide

Nurido may process information that you voluntarily enter into the Application, including:

  • Age, gender, height, current weight, and target weight
  • Activity level and dietary preferences
  • Health and nutrition goals
  • Daily calorie and macronutrient targets
  • Food and meal records
  • Weight tracking history
  • Application language preferences and UI settings

This information is used exclusively to provide core functionality, such as personalized calorie targets, nutrition tracking, and progress visualization.

2.2 Information Stored Locally (No-Account Architecture)

Nurido operates primarily on a local-first architecture.

Nurido does not require user registration, login credentials, or account creation.

Your profile, meal logs, weight history, nutrition targets, and settings are stored locally on your device storage/sandbox.

This local data is not automatically synced, backed up, or uploaded to Nurido's proprietary servers.

2.3 Food Photos (AI Analysis)

When you voluntarily use the AI food analysis feature:

  • The selected or captured food image is transmitted securely via HTTPS/TLS to our backend service.
  • The image is processed to identify food items and estimate nutritional values (e.g., calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats).
  • We do not sell your food photos, use them for targeted advertising, or use them to train machine learning/AI models.

2.4 AI Coach Messages

When you voluntarily interact with the AI Coach:

  • The text prompt or query you submit is transmitted securely to our backend service to generate the requested response.
  • We do not sell your AI Coach messages, use them for marketing or advertising, or use them to train machine learning/AI models.

3. How We Use Information

Nurido processes data solely for the following operational purposes:

  • Calculating personalized calorie and nutrition targets
  • Tracking meals, weight, and nutritional progress
  • Processing food images and text queries via AI
  • Providing multilingual localization
  • Diagnosing technical errors, crashes, and app stability
  • Processing optional Premium subscriptions and in-app purchases via Google Play Billing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or health-related data to any data brokers or third parties.

4. AI Processing & Google Cloud Infrastructure

Nurido utilizes Google Cloud Infrastructure and Google Gemini AI to deliver automated nutrition estimations and conversational coaching.

When you trigger an AI feature:

  • The food image or text prompt is sent via encrypted HTTPS/TLS to our backend proxy.
  • The backend securely queries the Gemini API to generate the requested analysis.
  • The response is returned to your device.

Important Health Disclaimer:

AI-generated nutrition estimates, food breakdowns, and AI Coach responses are provided for general educational and informational purposes only. AI models can produce inaccurate results. Nurido is not a medical device, and its outputs do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified physician or registered dietitian before making significant changes to your diet or health regimen.

5. Third-Party Services

Nurido uses third-party services to provide, maintain, analyze, and improve certain features of the Application. These third-party services may collect or process information in accordance with their own Privacy Policies.

Third-party services used by Nurido include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase services
  • Google Gemini API

These services may process information necessary to provide their respective functionality. For more information about how these services handle information, please refer to their privacy policies linked above.

6. Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

Firebase Analytics: Collects non-personally identifiable metrics (such as screen views, session duration, app version, and general device specs) to help us understand feature adoption and improve user experience.

Firebase Crashlytics: Collects stack traces, device state, and error logs when a crash occurs to help us resolve software bugs.

When you initiate the in-app Delete My Data action, Nurido automatically resets the Firebase App Instance ID, unsets user properties, and deletes pending local crash logs.

7. Device Permissions

Nurido requests runtime permissions strictly when needed for a user-initiated action:

  • Camera (CAMERA): Required only if you take a live photo of a meal for AI analysis.
  • Photos/Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Storage): Required only if you select an existing meal photo from your gallery.

You can grant, modify, or revoke these permissions at any time via your device settings (Settings > Apps > Nurido > Permissions).

8. Notifications

Nurido may provide optional local reminders (e.g., meal logging reminders, water tracking, target alerts). These notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notifications for third-party advertisements or marketing broadcasts.

9. Premium Subscriptions & Financial Data

Purchases of Premium features are handled entirely by Google Play In-App Billing.

Nurido does not collect, view, process, or store:

  • Credit/debit card numbers
  • Billing addresses
  • Banking or payment credentials

All transactions are governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Nurido receives only purchase validation tokens to unlock purchased features.

10. Data Security

In-Transit Protection: All network communications with our backend and AI services are encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

On-Device Isolation: Locally stored data relies on the Android sandbox security architecture, preventing other standard applications on your device from accessing your Nurido logs.

While we implement robust security safeguards, no method of digital transmission or local electronic storage is 100% immune to vulnerabilities.

11. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Health logs, weights, meal records, and settings remain on your device indefinitely until you manually delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • AI Ephemeral Processing: Food images and text submitted to our AI backend are processed ephemerally and are not permanently retained in our database.
  • Third-Party Telemetry: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data are retained in accordance with Google Firebase's standard retention cycles (typically 2 to 14 months).

12. Data Deletion & Account Deletion Compliance

12.1 No Account Required

Because Nurido uses a local-first model without centralized user accounts or cloud profiles, there is no remote account to deactivate.

12.2 In-App "Delete My Data"

Nurido provides a built-in "Delete My Data" option in the application settings. Triggering this feature will immediately:

  • Erase all locally stored health records, nutrition logs, targets, and user preferences from your device.
  • Reset the Firebase Analytics App Instance ID and remove custom user attributes.
  • Remove Crashlytics identifiers and purge unsent crash reports stored locally.

12.3 Manual Removal

You can also permanently remove all application data at any time by clearing storage via Android settings (Settings > Apps > Nurido > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.

13. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Regional Laws)

Depending on your country or region of residence (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California), you may have certain legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Since your data is stored locally on your device, you have direct access to view and export your logs within the application.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can erase your data at any time using the in-app "Delete My Data" button or by clearing application storage.
  • Right to Object / Restrict Processing: You can discontinue AI processing at any time by choosing not to use AI image or AI Coach features.
  • Sale of Personal Information: Nurido does not sell, rent, or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising (we do not sell your data under CCPA/CPRA).

To submit a privacy inquiry or exercise any applicable statutory rights, please contact us at support.nurido@gmail.com.

14. Children's Privacy

Nurido is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate remedial steps.

15. International Data Transfers

While user data remains primarily on your device, interactions with third-party cloud infrastructure (such as Google Cloud and Gemini AI) may involve secure transmission to servers located outside your home country. These transfers are governed by standard contractual clauses and Google's global data processing safeguards.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new feature rollouts, or evolving legal obligations.

When modifications occur, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
