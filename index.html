1. Introduction

Welcome to Nurido ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Hoang Dev.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. Nurido is designed as an offline-first, standalone health and nutrition assistant that operates primarily on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nurido processes, how your data is stored, how AI features function, and how your privacy is protected when using our mobile application.

By downloading, accessing, or using Nurido, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Storage & Processing

Nurido operates primarily on local device storage to ensure maximum privacy and user control over personal data.

A. No Account Registration Required

Nurido does not require account registration or sign-in. We do not create or maintain user accounts, and we do not collect account credentials such as email addresses or passwords for using the app.

B. Local Health & Profile Data (Stored on Your Device)

Information you voluntarily enter during setup or in your health profile is stored locally on your device using local storage technologies, including:

  • Height, weight, and target weight
  • Age and gender
  • Activity level and health goals
  • Daily calorie target, BMR, TDEE, and macronutrient targets (Protein, Carbs, Fat, Fiber)
  • Daily food and meal logs
  • Weight history
  • Language preferences
  • Application settings

C. Meal Photos & Text Submitted for AI Analysis

When you choose to capture a meal photo or send a message to the AI nutrition assistant, that specific content is securely transmitted via encrypted HTTPS for processing in order to generate nutrition analysis and AI responses.

3. How We Use Information

Your information is used solely to provide the core functionality of Nurido, including:

  • Calculating personalized calorie and nutrition targets
  • Tracking meal history and weight progress locally
  • Providing AI-powered nutrition analysis
  • Delivering multilingual application experiences
  • Maintaining application stability and responding to support requests

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. AI Features

Nurido provides AI-powered nutrition analysis through a secure backend service.

When you use AI features:

  • Meal photos and text prompts that you submit are securely transmitted via encrypted HTTPS for processing.
  • Your data is processed only to generate the nutrition analysis and AI responses you request.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data for advertising or marketing purposes.

Important Health & Medical Disclaimer

AI-generated nutrition recommendations and food calorie estimates are provided for informational and educational purposes only and do not constitute medical advice.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian before making significant dietary or health decisions.

5. Device Permissions

Nurido requests access to your device's camera and photo library only when you choose to capture or select a food photo for AI analysis.

These permissions are optional and can be managed or revoked at any time through your device settings.

6. Notifications

Nurido may provide optional notifications to remind you about meals, nutrition goals, weight tracking, hydration, application updates, and other health-related features.

Notifications are entirely optional and can be enabled, disabled, or managed at any time through your device settings.

Nurido does not use notifications for advertising or third-party marketing purposes.

7. Third-Party Services

Nurido relies on trusted third-party infrastructure to provide AI functionality and application services.

These services process data only as necessary to provide the requested functionality and operate under their own privacy and security practices.

8. Data Security & Storage

Your health profile, meal history, and application settings are stored locally on your device.

Data transmitted for AI processing is protected using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption during transmission.

9. Data Retention & Local Data Deletion

Since your profile, meal history, weight records, and application settings are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over deleting your data at any time.

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Open Nurido.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Data.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

This permanently removes your locally stored health profile, meal history, weight records, and application settings from your device.

You can also uninstall Nurido or clear the application's storage through your device settings to permanently remove all locally stored data.

10. Children's Privacy

Nurido is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date.
